Artifact What it does Why it matters

Buyer follow-up prompts Extends the first answer into the most common follow-up questions around vendors, training stance, scope, proof, and approvals. Keeps the second reply from becoming another rewrite project.
Internal handoff worksheet Summarizes owner, blocker, open questions, next reviewer, and unresolved proof items in one note. Shortens cleanup before founder, privacy, or counsel review.

Packet-escalation guide Shows when to stay in one-answer mode versus when to widen into a packet, teardown, or paid review path. Stops teams from overbuilding too early or missing the broader handoff when it is truly needed.

Which tier to buy
