Starter artifact manifest

These are the operating files a Starter buyer receives during early access. The point is a usable one-change packet, not a vague “template bundle.”

Artifact What it does Why it matters
Subprocessor list template Captures vendor, purpose, data categories, region, dates, and owner in one row format. Keeps the public page and internal record aligned.
Objection-window tracker Tracks notice date, deadline, status, owner, and resolution. Prevents the deadline from living in an email thread only.
Evidence log template Stores proof links, send timestamps, and closeout notes. Gives procurement or counsel one place to inspect the trail.
Notice email variants Short, formal, and enterprise-oriented customer notice drafts. Reduces first-draft friction when the change becomes time-sensitive.
Internal approval checklist Lists the minimum facts and approvals to lock before send. Helps founders avoid shipping half-ready notice copy.
Attorney-review handoff note Summarizes the change, assumptions, and open questions for review. Shortens cleanup work before legal review.

Pro artifact manifest

Pro expands the workflow for teams with repeated vendor changes, multiple customer segments, or counsel/procurement review expectations.

Artifact What it adds Best fit
Multi-change vendor register Tracks proposed, active, replaced, and removed vendors across changes. Teams juggling more than one vendor change.
Customer notice matrix Maps segment, deadline, owner, method, and status in one place. Teams with different DPAs or notice rules by customer group.
DPA clause intake worksheet Captures notice periods, special recipients, and escalation notes. Founders or advisors cleaning up contract-specific variation.
CSV import and export guide Shows how to carry the workflow between local files and operating sheets. Teams standardizing the process for reuse.
Attorney-review packet Packages the change, notice draft, evidence links, and open questions. Teams that need a tighter handoff into review.
Procurement-ready summary and 90-day calendar Keeps the story and deadlines visible after the first send. Teams feeling ongoing enterprise or renewal pressure.
