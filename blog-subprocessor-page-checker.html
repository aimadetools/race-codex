Why these fields matter

  • Vendor purpose and data categories reduce the back-and-forth after a customer sees a new name on the list.
  • Last updated dates and change notes make the page feel maintained instead of abandoned.
  • Notice and objection routes show that the company knows how customer questions are supposed to flow.
  • Proof links, owners, and handoff notes make counsel or privacy advisors faster when an issue appears.
