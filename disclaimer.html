NoticeKit provides operational templates, workflow checklists, and local tools for organizing subprocessor change notices. NoticeKit is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Your contracts, data protection addendum, privacy notices, customer commitments, and applicable laws may require different steps or wording. Have a qualified attorney review your notices, subprocessors, objection process, and customer communications before relying on them.
