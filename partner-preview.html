Partner preview

Referral and white-label workflow for SaaS privacy advisors.

NoticeKit gives consultants, fractional DPOs, startup attorneys, and compliance operators a narrow operating kit for client subprocessor change notices. It organizes facts, drafts, trackers, and evidence for review without claiming to provide legal advice.

Who this is for

The partner version is designed for advisors who already help small SaaS teams prepare privacy operations, vendor inventories, DPA reviews, procurement responses, or security readiness work.

Fractional DPOs

Use the Pro workflow to capture vendor facts, segment customers, and prepare attorney-ready evidence packets before a client sends notice.

Privacy consultants

Add a repeatable subprocessor notice workflow to privacy program setup, vendor inventory cleanup, or enterprise-sales readiness projects.

Startup attorneys

Receive cleaner client facts, dates, contract assumptions, and open questions before legal review. No fee-sharing is assumed.

What partners can show clients

The preview is based on the Pro kit manifest and focuses on operational deliverables, not legal conclusions.

Artifact Client use Partner value
Multi-change vendor register Track proposed, active, replaced, and removed subprocessors. Turns scattered vendor context into one reviewable operating record.
Customer notice matrix Segment customers by notice method, deadline, owner, and status. Helps advisors spot missing recipients and unmanaged objection windows.
DPA clause intake worksheet Capture notice periods, special recipients, and escalation notes. Separates business facts from legal interpretation before counsel review.
Attorney-review packet Summarize the change, assumptions, evidence, and open review questions. Reduces back-and-forth when legal review is needed.
Evidence folder workflow Store notice copy, approval proof, page proof, and objection logs locally. Creates a practical audit trail without requiring hosted NoticeKit storage.
