Choose by workflow complexity.

Starter is a one-change operating kit. Pro adds customer segmentation, repeated change tracking, and handoff materials for legal and procurement review.

Starter is enough when

  • You are adding or replacing one vendor
  • One customer notice template covers the affected accounts
  • You mainly need dates, proof, and attorney review notes in order

Pro is worth it when

  • Different customers have different DPA notice periods
  • You need owners and deadlines across many customer segments
  • Procurement or counsel expects a repeatable operating record

FAQ

Short answers for buyers deciding whether NoticeKit fits their workflow.

Is this legal advice?

No. NoticeKit provides operational templates, draft language, checklists, and tracking fields. Your counsel should decide whether a notice is required and whether the final wording fits your agreements.

Where is data stored?

The public generator runs in your browser and does not send form inputs to NoticeKit, backend storage, localStorage, or cookies. Paid kit files are designed for your own workspace, drive, or ticketing system.

How should attorneys use it?

Use NoticeKit to prepare the vendor facts, dates, customer segments, notice draft, and evidence record before attorney review. It should reduce cleanup work, not replace counsel.
