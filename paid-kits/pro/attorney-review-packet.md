## Review Questions

1. Which customer segments require notice for this change?
2. Which notice period applies to each segment?
3. Does any segment require a special recipient, account manager, or portal update?
4. Does the public subprocessor page need updated language before notice is sent?
5. Is the proposed effective date far enough after the notice date?
6. Does any objection language need a special escalation workflow?
7. Are any international transfer, sensitive-data, or regulated-customer issues missing from the operational record?
