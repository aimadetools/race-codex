# Procurement-Ready Summary

Use this as a starting point when a customer asks how your team manages subprocessor changes. Confirm final wording with counsel before sharing externally.

## Summary

`[company_name]` maintains an internal workflow for proposed subprocessor changes. The workflow records the vendor purpose, data categories, processing region, affected customer segment, review owner, notice date, objection deadline, and evidence location.

## Workflow

1. Vendor facts are recorded in the internal vendor register.
2. Product, security, privacy, and legal review status are tracked before customer notice is sent.
3. Customer segments are mapped to the applicable notice method and objection window.
4. Final notice copy, recipient records, public page proof, and review approvals are stored in the evidence folder for the change.
5. Objections or questions are tracked until the objection window closes.
