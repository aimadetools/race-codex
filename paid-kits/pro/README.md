# NoticeKit Pro

Pro includes the Starter files plus repeatable workflows for multiple vendor changes, customer segments, DPA terms, and evidence records.

## Files

- `multi-change-vendor-register.csv`: register for proposed, active, replaced, and removed subprocessors.
- `customer-notice-matrix.csv`: segment-level notice tracking by owner, method, deadline, and status.
- `dpa-clause-intake-worksheet.csv`: structured worksheet for customer or segment notice terms.
- `attorney-review-packet.md`: reusable packet for counsel review.
- `procurement-ready-summary.md`: concise customer-facing workflow explanation.
- `90-day-operating-calendar.csv`: operating calendar for page updates, notices, reminders, and closeout.
- `csv-import-export-guide.md`: CSV rules for browser-local and spreadsheet workflows.
- `evidence-folder-workflow.md`: folder structure for preserving proof without NoticeKit hosting.
- `07-closeout-note.md`: archive summary for the final proof set after the objection window closes.

## Upgrade Use Case

Use Pro when different customer groups have different notice periods, owners, evidence requirements, or escalation paths. The goal is to avoid running a new improvised process for every vendor change.

## Disclaimer

NoticeKit provides operational templates, workflow checklists, and local tools for organizing subprocessor change notices. NoticeKit is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Your contracts, data protection addendum, privacy notices, customer commitments, and applicable laws may require different steps or wording. Have a qualified attorney review your notices, subprocessors, objection process, and customer communications before relying on them.
