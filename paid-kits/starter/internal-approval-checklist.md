# Internal Approval Checklist

Use this checklist before sending a subprocessor change notice. Keep completed copies in the evidence folder for the change.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Fill these fields before the review checks start. If a field is unknown, mark it `follow-up` and name the owner who will resolve it.

- Change ID:
- Vendor:
- Change type: add / replace / remove / update
- Purpose:
- Data categories:
- Processing region:
- Customer segment:
- Notice date:
- Planned effective date:
- Objection deadline:
- Internal owner:

## Change Summary

Use the summary block to capture the final approved values once the checklist is complete.

## Operational Checks

- [ ] Vendor purpose is described in plain language and names the business reason for the change.
- [ ] Data categories are specific enough for customer review and do not use vague labels like "customer data".
- [ ] Processing region is known or clearly marked for follow-up.
- [ ] Public subprocessor page entry is drafted or linked in the evidence folder.
- [ ] Customer segment is identified.
- [ ] Notice method is known for the affected customer or segment.
- [ ] Recipient list source is recorded and points to the actual export, account list, or CRM segment.
- [ ] Evidence folder is created with the standard `YYYY-MM-DD_vendor-name_change-type` naming pattern.

## Review Checks

- [ ] Product owner confirms the vendor is needed for the stated business reason.
- [ ] Security owner confirms vendor security review status.
- [ ] Privacy owner confirms data categories and region.
- [ ] Customer success or founder confirms customer segment.
- [ ] Counsel reviews whether notice is required and whether wording is appropriate.

## Send Checks

- [ ] Final notice copy is saved in the evidence folder.
- [ ] Public page update proof is saved in the evidence folder.
- [ ] Notice sender and send date are recorded.
- [ ] Objection deadline is recorded in the tracker.
- [ ] Follow-up reminder is scheduled before the deadline.
- [ ] Closeout owner is assigned.

## Open Questions For Counsel

- Does this change trigger notice under the relevant DPA or customer agreement?
- Does the customer have an objection right?
- Is advance notice required before the effective date?
- Does this change require different handling for specific countries, enterprise accounts, or regulated customers?
- Does the final notice need any contract-specific language?

## Completion Standard

Treat the checklist as complete only when the approval checks, send checks, and evidence folder all point to the same change ID, vendor, notice date, and objection deadline.
