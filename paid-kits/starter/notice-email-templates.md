This vendor may process `[data_categories]` for `[customer_segment]`. Processing may occur in `[processing_region]`.

Planned effective date: `[effective_date]`
Objection deadline: `[objection_deadline]`

## Attorney Review Questions

- Does the agreement require notice for this customer or segment?
- Is the objection deadline calculated correctly?
- Does the notice need additional international transfer language?
- Should any customer segment receive a different version?
- Should the public subprocessor page be updated before, at the same time as, or after notice?
