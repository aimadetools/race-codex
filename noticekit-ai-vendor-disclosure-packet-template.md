## 5. Reviewer Questions To Answer Fast

- Which vendors power the AI feature, hosting, analytics, support, auth, and billing flow?
- Which customers are actually affected by the vendor change?
- Does the public vendor list match the live production stack?
- Is direct customer notice required, or is the packet still waiting on contract review?
- Who owns send, objections, proof capture, and closeout?
