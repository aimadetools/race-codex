AI route choice

Use the starter pack when the next step is fuzzy. Use the builder when one answer is already blocking the deal.

The answer builder is the better first click when one live questionnaire row needs copy-ready wording now or when the sheet is ready for a first-pass response pack. This page separates those jobs so the team does not overbuy or stall on the wrong artifact.

When starter pack wins

The blocker is still fuzzy

You know there is a buyer review, but you do not yet know whether the real fix is one answer, repeat-review support, proof, or control-boundary work.

You want one clean route

The team needs a fast way to compare the builder, example, template, packet, teardown, evidence, or agent-control paths without bouncing between tabs.

You are still deciding scope

The buyer thread is live, but the team has not yet decided whether the next step is Starter, Pro, or an async audit.

When the spreadsheet-template guide wins

The sheet itself is the blocker

The buyer already sent questionnaire rows or a portal export, and the immediate job is preserving row wording, section logic, reviewer lane, and answer direction before the team drafts anything.

You still need to choose builder vs bank

The worksheet needs cleanup first, but the team has not yet decided whether the next move is a one-answer builder pass, a reusable answer bank, or a broader software comparison.

You want to keep the row logic visible

The spreadsheet route keeps the downloadable template, handoff path, builder, and answer-bank branch visible instead of forcing the work into a finished paragraph too early.

When builder wins

One question is blocking the deal

The reviewer already asked for the answer and the team just needs clean wording, proof context, a handoff note, or a first-pass response set for exact spreadsheet or portal rows.

You need more than the answer

The live thread also needs a reviewer workspace export, answer-bank draft, follow-up pack, pasted-question response pack, and a local bundle the team can reuse later.

You want something concrete to send

The builder is the fastest way to turn the current facts into a response the team can paste without rebuilding the whole review in Slack or email.

Do not force software-shopping buyers back into a one-answer page.

If the team is already comparing approved-answer workflow, answer libraries, or SME review software, jump to the response-software guide. If the debate has shifted into assignments, approvals, governance, or broader queue administration, jump to the management-software guide before you keep arguing about starter pack versus builder.
