When starter pack wins

The blocker is still fuzzy

You know there is a buyer review, but you do not yet know whether the real fix is one answer, repeat-review support, proof, or control-boundary work.

You want one clean route

The team needs a fast way to compare the builder, example, template, packet, teardown, evidence, or agent-control paths without bouncing between tabs.

You are still deciding scope

The buyer thread is live, but the team has not yet decided whether the next step is Starter, Pro, or an async audit.

When builder wins

One question is blocking the deal

The reviewer already asked for the answer and the team just needs clean wording, proof context, and a handoff note.

You need more than the answer

The live thread also needs a reviewer workspace export, answer-bank draft, follow-up pack, and a local bundle the team can reuse later.

You want something concrete to send

The builder is the fastest way to turn the current facts into a response the team can paste without rebuilding the whole review in Slack or email.
