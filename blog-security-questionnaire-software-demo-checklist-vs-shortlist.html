The common mistake: letting the calls replace the recommendation

Teams often treat polished demos as a substitute for route clarity or vendor ranking. That makes presentation quality feel more important than actual queue fit. The shortlist worksheet exists to sharpen the recommendation first. The demo checklist exists to keep the later calls honest by forcing every feature claim back to the blocker, proof burden, and rollout risk already on the table.

Practical sequence.

Use the software fit scorecard first if the lane is still fuzzy. Use the shortlist worksheet when vendor names are already live and the team needs a ranked recommendation. Use the demo checklist once the top options are real and the next useful step is a pressure-tested call brief.
