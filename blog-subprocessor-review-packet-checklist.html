Subprocessor review packet checklist for startup attorneys

When a founder sends you a vendor-change notice to review, the fastest path is a compact packet that includes the facts, the draft copy, and the proof trail in one place.

Operational checklist, not legal advice.

This page helps teams prepare a cleaner review packet for counsel or a fractional DPO. It does not tell you what the right legal answer is for a specific agreement.

What belongs in the packet

A good review packet should let an attorney confirm the notice facts quickly, compare them against the agreement, and decide whether the draft needs a narrower segment, a different deadline, or a stronger explanation.

Review packet checklist

Agreement facts

Include the DPA, the relevant customer agreement, the notice period, the objection language, and any recipient rules that affect who must receive the message.

Vendor facts

List the vendor name, service purpose, data categories, processing region, and whether this is a new vendor, a replacement, or a material change to an existing vendor.

Customer segment

Show which customers are affected and why. If the notice only applies to one region or contract group, make that scope obvious in the packet.

Draft notice copy

Attach the subject line, body copy, short version, effective date, and objection deadline so the reviewer can scan the exact wording without hunting through drafts.

Proof trail

Provide the archived subprocessor page, the evidence log, the sender, the intended delivery channel, and any internal approval notes already collected.

Open questions

Flag anything that still needs legal judgment, such as a shortened notice period, a sensitive region change, or an objection workflow that might need human follow-up.

Fast review workflow

  • Verify the contract language that controls notice and objection timing.
  • Check whether the affected customer set is correctly segmented.
  • Confirm the data categories and processing region are stated in plain language.
  • Review the deadline, sender, and delivery method against the agreement.
  • Ask for the archived page and evidence log before approval so the record is complete at send time.

What to send back to the founder

Subject: Subprocessor notice review complete

Thanks. The packet is complete enough for review. I checked the agreement language, the affected segment, the draft notice, the deadline fields, and the evidence trail.

Before sending, please confirm {{open_question_1}} and {{open_question_2}}. If those are resolved, the notice can go out with the current draft.

Why this helps the review cycle

Less back-and-forth

A tighter packet cuts down on the common request to resend the DPA, the notice draft, or the evidence source separately.

Cleaner approval record

When the packet already contains the facts, the final approval note is easier to archive and later explain to a customer or auditor.
