Sample generated notice copy

The local preview turns a vendor change into customer-facing draft language your team can review before sending.

Subject: Upcoming subprocessor update

We are updating our subprocessor list to add Acme Email Cloud for transactional email delivery. This vendor may process customer names and email addresses for EU customers on signed DPAs. Processing may occur in the United States and European Union.

Notice date: April 21, 2026
Planned effective date: May 21, 2026
Objection deadline: May 21, 2026

If your agreement includes a right to object, please send any objection within 30 days of this notice so we can review it before the change takes effect.
