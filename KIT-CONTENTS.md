# NoticeKit Paid Kit Contents

This document defines the paid download contents so Starter and Pro feel materially different before checkout links are connected.

## Packaging Rules

- Deliver files as editable Google Sheets, Excel-compatible CSVs, Google Docs, and PDF exports.
- Keep all customer, vendor, and contract data local to the buyer's workspace.
- Position every artifact as operational preparation for counsel, not legal advice.
- Include a short "how to use this with your attorney" note in each paid tier.

## Starter - $29 One-Time

Starter is for a founder or operator adding one vendor and needing a clean notice workflow this week.

### Buyer Trigger

- One upcoming subprocessor addition or replacement.
- A DPA or customer contract mentions advance notice or objection rights.
- The team needs a customer-ready draft and an internal proof record, but not bulk customer tracking.

### Included Files

| File | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subprocessor list template | Google Sheet, XLSX, CSV | Track vendor name, service, data categories, region, public URL, effective date, and last reviewed date. |
| Customer notice email templates | Google Doc, PDF | Provide short, formal, and enterprise-account variants for announcing one vendor change. |
| Objection window tracker | Google Sheet, XLSX | Calculate notice date, deadline, owner, status, and follow-up notes for a single change. |
| Internal approval checklist | Google Doc, PDF | Confirm vendor owner, contract basis, security review, privacy review, and counsel review before sending. |
| Evidence log template | Google Sheet, XLSX | Preserve proof of notice, recipients, send date, page update URL, and reviewer initials. |
| Attorney-review handoff note | Google Doc, PDF | Give counsel the facts, dates, assumptions, and open questions in one place. |

### Boundaries

- No CSV import/export workflow.
- No multi-customer segmentation matrix.
- No board or procurement summary.
- No white-label consultant rights.

## Pro - $79 One-Time

Pro is for a team managing several customers, multiple notice obligations, or repeated vendor updates.

### Buyer Trigger

- The team sells into enterprise or EU accounts with different DPA terms.
- More than one customer segment needs notice tracking.
- Operations, legal, or customer success needs a reusable workflow instead of a one-off email.

### Included Files

| File | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everything in Starter | Mixed | Preserve all one-change templates and review artifacts. |
| Multi-change vendor register | Google Sheet, XLSX, CSV | Track proposed, active, replaced, and removed subprocessors across repeated changes. |
| CSV import/export guide | PDF, CSV examples | Explain stable column names, date formats, and spreadsheet-safe values for local tools. |
| Customer notice matrix | Google Sheet, XLSX | Track customer segment, contract basis, notice method, sent status, objection deadline, and owner. |
| DPA clause intake worksheet | Google Sheet, XLSX | Capture notice periods, objection language, special recipients, and escalation notes by customer or segment. |
| Attorney-review packet | Google Doc, PDF | Summarize the change, supporting evidence, customer impact, and review questions for counsel. |
| Procurement-ready summary | Google Doc, PDF | Convert the workflow into a concise customer-facing explanation for security and procurement reviews. |
| 90-day operating calendar | Google Sheet, XLSX | Plan page updates, customer notices, objection windows, reminders, and evidence closeout. |
| Evidence folder workflow | PDF, folder template | Organize notice copy, approvals, recipient records, page proof, and objection logs without backend storage. |

### Upgrade Value

Pro adds repeatability and segmentation. Starter answers "can we send this notice correctly?" Pro answers "can we run this process across customers without losing deadlines or evidence?"

## Concierge Audit - $249 One-Time

Concierge Audit is a manual service, not a downloadable kit.

### Scope

- Review one public subprocessor page or equivalent vendor disclosure.
- Review one current notice workflow or draft customer notice.
- Identify missing dates, vague processor descriptions, evidence gaps, and objection-window risks.
- Return a prioritized remediation plan within 48 hours.

### Deliverable

- Annotated findings memo.
- Prioritized fix list.
- Recommended Starter or Pro workflow setup.
- Optional 30-minute handoff call if capacity allows.
