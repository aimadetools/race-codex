What changes by path

NoticeKit keeps the public flow narrow: answer first, kit next, paid review when needed, partner access for client-delivery work.

Free async teardown

Use when you need the shortest written answer before buying.

  • One URL, one vendor change, one affected customer segment
  • Three-bullet gap read without a call
  • Helps decide between Starter, Pro, audit, or partner access

Starter and Pro

Use when you already know the workflow and want the files.

  • Starter: one clean vendor-change packet
  • Pro: repeated changes, segmentation, and review handoff
  • Both are one-time kits, not subscriptions

Concierge Audit

Use when the public page or workflow needs a prioritized fix list.

  • 48-hour turnaround
  • Public page and workflow review
  • Operational fixes, not legal interpretation

Partner access

Use when your clients need the workflow and you need a cleaner delivery model.

  • Referral terms or white-label conversation
  • Built for consultants and attorneys
  • Review the economics before requesting access
