Use this temporary intake while checkout and the permanent contact domain are being connected. The page builds an email draft locally; no form data is stored by NoticeKit.

What to include

  • Company name and website
  • Current subprocessor page URL
  • Vendor being added, removed, or replaced
  • Planned effective date and customer notice window
  • Customer segment affected by the change
  • Anything counsel has already asked you to preserve

NoticeKit will use the details to prepare an operational gap list. It does not interpret your contracts or provide legal advice.
