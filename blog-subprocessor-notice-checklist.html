Checklist

Subprocessor notice checklist for SaaS teams

Use this before you send a vendor-change notice so you do not miss the facts, the timing, or the proof your customer or counsel will later ask for.

Operational checklist, not legal advice.

This page helps teams prepare a notice workflow. It does not decide whether a specific notice is required under your DPA or customer agreement.

When to use this checklist

Run the checklist when you add a new subprocessor, replace a vendor, change the processing region, or update the purpose a vendor serves in the product.

The point is to turn a messy vendor change into a repeatable workflow you can review before customers see it.

Checklist

  • Confirm the vendor legal name and public website.
  • Describe the service purpose in customer-facing language.
  • List the data categories that may be processed.
  • Note the processing region or transfer context.
  • Capture the notice date and planned effective date.
  • Calculate the objection deadline from the controlling agreement.
  • Identify the affected customer segment or contract source.
  • Assign an internal owner and reviewer.
  • Save the final notice copy and page snapshot in one evidence folder.

Example checklist entry

Vendor: Acme Email Cloud

Purpose: Transactional email delivery

Data: Customer names and email addresses

Region: United States and European Union

Notice date: 2026-04-21

Effective date: 2026-05-21

Objection deadline: 2026-05-21

Status: Ready for attorney review

What gets missed most often

No deadline math

If the notice does not clearly show the objection window, customers cannot tell when the change becomes effective.

No evidence folder

Notice copy, screenshot proof, and reviewer notes should live together before the change ships.

No customer segment

One vendor change may not apply to every customer. Segment by agreement or product line before sending.

No reviewer signoff

Even small teams need to know who checked the wording before the notice goes out.
