# NoticeKit Buyer Validation Packet

Date: 2026-04-20

## Objective

Validate whether small SaaS teams will pay for an operational subprocessor change notice workflow before NoticeKit spends more sessions on hosted monitoring, subscriptions, or complex product code.

The unresolved validation target is:

- 5 SaaS founders or operators.
- 3 fractional DPOs or privacy consultants.
- 2 startup attorneys or legal ops advisors.

## Interview Rules

- Keep calls to 15 minutes.
- Ask about a real recent or upcoming vendor change.
- Do not give legal advice or review contract meaning.
- Do not lead with the product demo. First understand the current workflow.
- End with a concrete buying or referral question.

## Founder Interview Script

1. What triggered your current privacy, DPA, or subprocessor work?
2. When you add a vendor, where do you record the vendor name, purpose, data categories, processing region, and effective date?
3. Does any customer agreement require advance notice or an objection window for new subprocessors?
4. Who owns the notice workflow: founder, engineering, ops, legal, DPO, or customer success?
5. What evidence would you show if a customer asked when they were notified?
6. What is painful about the current process?
7. What would make a $29 one-change kit useful enough to buy?
8. When would a $249 48-hour audit be easier than doing it yourself?
9. What would make you distrust this product?
10. Can I follow up when checkout is live, or is there someone else who owns this?

## Consultant Interview Script

1. How often do clients ask about subprocessors, DPAs, trust pages, or vendor-change notices?
2. What size or maturity of client has this problem most often?
3. Do clients usually need legal language, operational records, or both?
4. What is missing from their current spreadsheets or trust pages?
5. Would a repeatable intake packet save you time, or would it create review risk?
6. What would you need before using this with a client?
7. Would referral revenue, white-label use, or a client-ready bundle be more attractive?
8. What disclaimer or boundary language would you require?
9. What price would feel acceptable for a small SaaS client?
10. Can you introduce one founder who recently had this problem?

## Attorney Interview Script

1. Where do small SaaS teams usually fail operationally after they have a DPA?
2. Is a structured notice/evidence workflow useful for attorney review?
3. Which fields should be captured before counsel reviews a new subprocessor notice?
4. What language should NoticeKit avoid on the public site and in templates?
5. Would you prefer clients bring a completed intake packet or raw screenshots/spreadsheets?
6. What mistakes make subprocessor notice workflows risky?
7. Would you ever refer a client to an operational template kit?
8. What price point is too low to be credible or too high for this scope?
9. What should be in an attorney-review handoff note?
10. Can I quote your feedback anonymously in product decisions?

## Scoring

Score each interview from 0 to 2.

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | Hypothetical | Occasional nuisance | Active blocker or recurring pain |
| Urgency | No timeline | This quarter | Current vendor change, deal, audit, or review |
| Current workaround | None | Spreadsheet or ad hoc docs | Manual workflow that is painful but important |
| Buyer clarity | No owner | Shared owner | Named buyer with budget |
| Willingness to pay | Free only | Would pay $29 | Would pay $79+ or audit |
| Referral value | No intros | Maybe later | Offers a specific intro |

An interview is validation-positive if it scores 8 or higher and includes either willingness to pay or a specific referral.

## Decision Gates

After 5 founder interviews:

- If 3 or more are validation-positive, keep founders as primary and push for first paid checkout.
- If 1-2 are validation-positive but consultants are enthusiastic, package a consultant-facing Pro workflow next.
- If 0 are validation-positive, pause product build and revisit the problem framing.

After 10 total interviews:

- Build hosted monitoring only if at least 4 people ask for reminders, periodic checks, or recurring monitoring unprompted.
- Build more templates if buyers mostly ask for attorney-review readiness.
- Push concierge audits if urgency clusters around active enterprise reviews.
