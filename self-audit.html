    What the score means

    A high score does not decide whether a notice is legally required. It shows whether the operational packet is organized enough for a clean send and review.

    0-4

    High-risk gap

    Do not send from memory. Build the vendor facts, customer segment, timing, and proof record first.

    5-7

    Needs cleanup

    The workflow is close, but missing fields can create deadline confusion or weak evidence later.

    8-10

    Review-ready

    The operational record is ready for final attorney, DPO, or accountable operator review before sending.
