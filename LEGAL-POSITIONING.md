# NoticeKit Legal Positioning

This document defines the disclaimer and positioning language used across NoticeKit. It is operational copy for the product and should be reviewed by a licensed attorney before paid launch.

## Core Position

NoticeKit helps small SaaS teams organize subprocessor change facts, notice drafts, objection-window dates, and evidence records. It does not interpret contracts, decide whether notice is legally required, or replace legal review.

## Required Disclaimer

Use this full disclaimer on the public disclaimer page, checkout fulfillment notes, downloadable kit readme files, and audit intake forms:

> NoticeKit provides operational templates, workflow checklists, and local tools for organizing subprocessor change notices. NoticeKit is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Your contracts, data protection addendum, privacy notices, customer commitments, and applicable laws may require different steps or wording. Have a qualified attorney review your notices, subprocessors, objection process, and customer communications before relying on them.

## Short Disclaimer

Use this in footers, pricing cards, and compact CTAs:

> Operational templates, not legal advice. Attorney review recommended before use.

## Product Boundaries

NoticeKit can say:

- "Prepare facts, dates, drafts, and evidence for attorney review."
- "Track notice windows and internal approvals in one workflow."
- "Generate customer-ready draft language your counsel can review."
- "Keep local records of what changed, who reviewed it, and when notice was sent."

NoticeKit should not say:

- "Comply with GDPR automatically."
- "Meet your DPA obligations without counsel."
- "Legally valid notice for every contract."
- "Avoid all privacy or procurement risk."
- "We review your contracts" unless an attorney-led service exists.

## Audit Positioning

Concierge Audit is an operational workflow review. It can identify missing dates, unclear owner assignments, stale page details, broken evidence records, and unclear handoff steps. It should not make legal conclusions about whether a customer contract has been satisfied.

Recommended audit language:

> The Concierge Audit reviews your public subprocessor page and notice workflow for operational gaps. It does not provide legal advice or contract interpretation. We organize findings so your team and counsel can decide the appropriate next step.
