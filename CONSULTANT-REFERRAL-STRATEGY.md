# NoticeKit Consultant Referral Strategy

Date: 2026-04-20

## Goal

Turn fractional DPOs, privacy consultants, startup attorneys, and compliance operators into a low-friction distribution channel for NoticeKit without implying that NoticeKit provides legal advice.

The partner motion should create two outcomes:

- Consultants can use NoticeKit as a repeatable intake and evidence workflow for clients.
- NoticeKit can receive qualified buyer introductions from people who already see subprocessor notice pain.

## Best Partner Profiles

### Fractional DPOs and Privacy Consultants

These partners are the first priority because they often help small SaaS teams prepare privacy operations before enterprise reviews.

Good fit signals:

- Works with SaaS companies under 100 employees.
- Reviews DPAs, subprocessors, vendor inventories, or privacy operations.
- Publishes GDPR, vendor management, trust center, or compliance operations content.
- Offers productized audits, retainer support, or privacy program setup.

Offer:

- 25% referral commission on Starter and Pro sales for the first 12 months after each referred customer's first purchase.
- 15% referral commission on Concierge Audit sales.
- Optional white-label Pro kit rights for active partners who commit to using NoticeKit in client delivery.

### Startup Attorneys

Attorneys are useful referral partners, but the relationship must preserve legal boundaries.

Good fit signals:

- Represents B2B SaaS startups.
- Handles DPAs, privacy policies, vendor review, enterprise sales support, or security questionnaire work.
- Wants client facts organized before legal review.

Offer:

- No fee-sharing unless the attorney confirms it is permitted in their jurisdiction and under their professional obligations.
- Default to non-monetary referral tracking, client-ready intake files, and co-marketing.
- Provide an attorney-review handoff packet that makes NoticeKit clearly operational.

### Compliance Operators

Operators in founder communities, RevOps groups, security communities, and procurement-readiness networks can drive practical referrals.

Good fit signals:

- Helps teams pass customer security reviews.
- Runs SOC 2, ISO 27001, privacy, or enterprise-sales readiness projects.
- Sees vendor inventory and subprocessor page gaps during implementation.

Offer:

- 20% referral commission on Starter and Pro.
- $50 bounty for a converted Concierge Audit referral.
- Shared teardown content that points to operational gaps without shaming a company.

## White-Label Rights

White-label rights should be reserved for Pro-level partners, not included in the $79 public Pro kit.

### Included

- Use NoticeKit templates inside client delivery.
- Replace NoticeKit cover pages with the consultant's brand.
- Add consultant-specific implementation notes.
- Share completed client-specific files with that client.

### Excluded

- Reselling the raw template pack as a standalone product.
- Publishing the templates in a public resource library.
- Removing legal disclaimer language.
- Claiming NoticeKit or the consultant provides legal advice through the templates.
- Using NoticeKit branding in a way that implies a joint law firm, DPO service, or legal representation.

### Pricing

- Partner Starter Pack: free after an intro call and one qualified referral.
- White-Label Pro License: $199 one-time during early access.
- Agency License: $499 one-time for up to 10 client uses during early access.

These prices should be revisited after the first three partner conversations.

## Qualification Checklist

Before approving white-label rights, confirm:

- The partner serves B2B SaaS or startup clients.
- The partner understands NoticeKit is operational tooling, not legal advice.
- The partner agrees to preserve disclaimer language.
- The partner has a plausible path to at least two client referrals.
- The partner will not publish or resell the raw templates as their own product.
