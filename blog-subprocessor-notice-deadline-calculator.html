When the deadline looks wrong

  • If the effective date is earlier than the calculated deadline, move the change date or send the notice sooner.
  • If different customer segments use different notice periods, calculate each segment separately instead of sharing one deadline.
  • If your agreement uses business days, receipt dates, or portal delivery rules, flag the segment for manual review.
  • If the notice method changes, save proof of the delivery channel alongside the deadline math.
