What to include for Concierge Audit

The faster the intake is complete, the faster the audit can focus on the missing field, the review blocker, or the evidence trail instead of reconstructing the basics.

Live page or question

Send the current questionnaire prompt, AI stack page, public trust page, or review packet URL so the audit starts from the live source of truth.

Workflow details

Name the vendors in scope, the workflow being reviewed, and the customer segment or contract class affected by the answer.

Deadline pressure

Share the procurement deadline, security review date, rollout date, or other timing pressure that makes the workflow urgent.
